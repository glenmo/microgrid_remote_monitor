The rule, right now

?Both batteries above 95 %
or
?Microgrid frequency above 50.2 Hz
and
?Generator is not running
and
?Surplus heating is switched on

The rule has to hold for 5 minutes before heating starts, and heating stops 10 minutes after it no longer holds, so passing clouds don't make the aircons cycle on and off.

Why the frequency?

When the SP Pro's batteries are full it nudges the microgrid's AC frequency up from 50 Hz. Solar inverters see that and throttle back. A frequency above 50.2 Hz is a clear sign that sunshine is being turned away, so it's a good moment to use it.

What does it switch on?

Only units that are switched off, set to heat at the chosen °C. If someone is already using an aircon, it is left alone.

What happens when the surplus ends?

Ten minutes later, the automation turns off the units it switched on. If anyone changed one of those units in the meantime, it's treated as theirs and left running.

Why not the generator?

Energy from the generator is never "spare", so surplus heating never runs while the generator is on.

What isn't shown?

The Caretaker's Flat has its own aircon, but it's someone's home, so it isn't published here and isn't part of the automation.
